Delete your 20 Vision Crew Connect account

20 Vision Crew Connect is operated by 20 Vision Electric Inc., Ontario, Canada.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Email info@20visionelectric.com from the email address you use to sign in to Crew Connect.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Crew Connect account" and include your full name.
  3. We confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days. We'll email you when it's done.

If you only want some of your data removed (for example your profile photo, social media handles or chat messages), say so in the same email and we'll remove just that.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password) and face/fingerprint setup records
  • Your profile: photo, phone number, position, social media handles
  • Location points recorded while you were clocked in
  • Your chat messages, notifications and daily-log photos

What we have to keep

If you worked for 20 Vision Electric Inc., Ontario's Employment Standards Act, 2000 requires us to keep employee records such as your name, dates of employment, hours worked, timesheets and wage records for 3 years after they were made (some records, like the employee information form, for 3 years after your employment ends). We keep only those records, store them securely, and delete them when the legal period ends.
