Privacy Policy: 20 Vision Crew Connect

Effective November 22, 2026 · Operated by 20 Vision Electric Inc., Ontario, Canada

20 Vision Crew Connect ("the app") is an internal workforce app used by employees of 20 Vision Electric Inc. to clock in and out, submit timesheets, see schedules and message the crew. It is not intended for the general public. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Profile information you enter: name, company, position, apprentice level or licence, hire date, phone number, email address, profile photo, and any social media handles you choose to add.
  • Work time records: clock-in and clock-out times, job site, hours, lunch deductions, overtime, time-off requests, timesheet signatures and submitted timesheets.
  • Location: your device's precise location only while you are clocked in. It is used to confirm you are at the job site, show the office where crew are working, warn you if location is turned off, and clock you out automatically if location stays off for 5 minutes or you are more than the set distance (10 km by default) from the job site. On Android a notification is shown the whole time this is running. Location collection stops when you clock out.
  • Daily logs and photos: notes, safety reports and job photos you choose to add.
  • Messages: group and one-to-one chat messages sent in the app.
  • Device information: a record that face or fingerprint unlock was set up on a phone, and the phone type.

Face and fingerprint data never leave your phone. The app asks your phone's own face or fingerprint system to confirm it's you; the app and 20 Vision Electric never receive or store your face or fingerprint.

Why we use it

To record hours worked, prepare payroll, schedule work, keep crews safe on site, and communicate with employees. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. The owner and authorized HR or office staff of 20 Vision Electric Inc. can see employee records, locations while clocked in, timesheets and logs. Your name, photo and position are visible to other crew members (for example in chat and schedules). Pay rates are visible to the office only.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), with the database located in Toronto, Canada. Data is encrypted in transit. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, and job-site addresses are looked up through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

How long we keep it

Employment and payroll records are kept for as long as you work with us and afterwards for as long as required by law (in Ontario, records of hours and wages must generally be kept for at least three years). Live location for the current shift is removed when you clock out; the clock-in and clock-out points are kept with that shift's time record.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • You can turn off location or notifications in your phone settings. While clocked in, turning location off will lead to an automatic clock-out after two warnings.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, subject to records the law requires us to keep, by emailing info@20visionelectric.com.

Changes

We will update this page if the way the app handles information changes, and change the effective date above.
