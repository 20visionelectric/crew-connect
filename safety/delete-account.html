20 Vision Site Safety – Delete your account or data

This page is for users of the 20 Vision Site Safety app by 20 Vision Electric Inc.

How to ask

  1. Email info@20visionelectric.com from the email address you use in the app.
  2. Use the subject Site Safety account deletion (or Site Safety data deletion if you only want some data removed) and say what you want deleted.
  3. We will confirm it is you and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, profile (address, phone, emergency contact, profile photo) and the certificates you uploaded.

What we must keep

Safety records that Ontario law or the COR program require us to keep (daily safety checks, inspections, toolbox talk attendance, incident reports and policy sign-offs) are kept for the required period — at least one year after the project ends, and five years for workplace violence and harassment records — then deleted.
