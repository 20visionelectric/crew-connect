20 Vision Site Safety – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

20 Vision Site Safety is an internal health and safety app used by employees, co-op students and subcontractors of 20 Vision Electric Inc. (Ontario, Canada). It keeps the safety records the company needs under the Ontario Occupational Health and Safety Act and for the IHSA Certificate of Recognition (COR) program.

What we collect

  • Account and profile: name, email, phone, job title, date hired, home address, emergency contact and an optional profile photo.
  • Training records: photos or PDFs of certificates and their dates (for example working at heights, first aid, lift licence, WSIB documents).
  • Location: your location when you check in to or leave a job site, when you save a safety check, inspection, toolbox talk or incident report, and every 15 minutes while you are checked in and the app is open. Location is not collected when you are not checked in.
  • Photos: pictures you take of equipment, serial plates, inspection tags, incidents and certificates.
  • Safety records and signatures: daily safety checks, inspections, toolbox talk attendance, incident reports, corrective actions and company policy sign-offs, including your signature.

Why we collect it

Only to run the company's health and safety program: to prove safety checks were done on site, to keep equipment and training up to date, to report and investigate incidents as the law requires, and to show records to auditors and inspectors when requested.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. The 20 Vision Electric office (owner and authorized supervisors/HR) can see all records. Records may be shown to an IHSA auditor, a Ministry of Labour inspector, WSIB or others when the law requires. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and on your phone so the app works without signal. Data is encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

Safety records are kept for as long as the law and the COR program require (at least one year after a project ends; workplace violence and harassment records for five years) and then deleted.

Your choices

You can update your profile in the app. To ask for a copy of your information or to have it corrected or deleted (where the law allows), email info@20visionelectric.com.
